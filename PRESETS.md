# VESSEL factory bank

96 presets by iisben, generated from `Presets/FactoryBank.xml`. Every number in the QA columns is measured by `VesselCheck check`.

- **Loudness** is BS.1770-4 integrated, target −16 LUFS.
- **TP** is true peak in dBTP at the audition velocity. **Stress TP** is with velocity, mod wheel and aftertouch all at maximum.
- **Vel / MW / AT** are how far each source moves the timbre, in dB of level-normalised 1/3-octave spectrum (shape, movement or frame-to-frame flux). The pass mark is 1 dB.
- Amounts are the share of the destination's full range that the source sweeps at maximum.
- **Macros** are listed after each description in knob order: 1 tone, 2 motion, 3 dirt, 4 weight, 5 signature, 6 texture, 7 space, 8 transform. Each one changes the timbre by at least 1 dB and is loudness-matched.
